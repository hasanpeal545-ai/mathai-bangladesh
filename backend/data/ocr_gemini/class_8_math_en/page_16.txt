6.  ┌──┐  ┌──┬──┐  ┌──┬──┬──┬──┐

    1ˢᵗ fig.   2ⁿᵈ fig.      3ʳᵈ fig.
    A figure made with sticks of equal length has 88 sticks in the 15ᵗʰ figure.

    (a) Draw the next figure in the pattern and determine the number of sticks in it.
    (b) Form a formula to determine the number of sticks in the K-th figure, and find the 21ˢᵗ term.
    (c) Determine how many sticks are needed to construct the last 12 figures among the first 15 figures.

7.  Number pattern: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, …
    The k-th term of this number pattern is 3k.

    (a) Show that 69 and the number obtained by interchanging its digits add up to a multiple of 11.
    (b) Find the sum of the first 20 terms of the number pattern.
    (c) From the sum of every two consecutive terms of the number pattern, a new pattern is formed. Find the (k − 2)-th term of this new pattern.
